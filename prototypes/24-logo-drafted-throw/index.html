Lettering

SP is built like drafting lettering: straight strokes and arcs of a single 4-unit radius, square ends, the same weight everywhere. It sits on three faint guides (cap line, mid line, baseline) that are drawn first, as a draftsman would. No wobble.

The throw

A dashed flight line is drawn from outside the plate to the bowl of the P, with an amber target mark. The batarang follows that line, tip first, spinning, and lands in the target. The line and the mark then fade. Nothing shakes or bounces.

Scroll

Leaving the top pulls the batarang back out along its line, and only the cut mark is left. Back at the top, the flight line is drawn again and the batarang is thrown again. Reduced motion shows it lodged and still.
